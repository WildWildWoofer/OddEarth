CENTRAL THESIS

Evolution did not build one perfect sensor.
It built specialists.

Odd Earth treats every specialist as a biological proof-of-concept. The eagle, bat, dog, mole, shark and migratory bird each reveal a different answer to the same question:

What information exists here, and what kind of nervous system can learn to use it?
