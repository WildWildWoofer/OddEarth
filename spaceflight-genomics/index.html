Service delivery

A single longitudinal record spanning preparation, flight and recovery.

Pre-flight

Whole-genome interpretation, susceptibility map, personalized nutrition, baseline biomarkers and monitoring plan.

In-flight

Mission-specific physiology, cognition and performance tracking against the astronaut's own baseline.

Post-flight

Recovery trajectory, follow-up biomarkers and a longitudinal personal spaceflight record for future missions.

Evidence base

Human spaceflight evidence first; terrestrial functional genetics only where it maps cleanly onto a documented spaceflight pathway.
